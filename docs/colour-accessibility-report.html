Colour-Vision Deficiency — Before/After Chart Demonstrations

UNI·CEN Census Observatory · companion to COLOUR-ACCESSIBILITY-AUDIT.md · generated 2026-08-09

Each chart below is rendered with the app's real ECharts option structures and synthetic census-like data, then shown through colour-blindness simulation filters (Machado et al. 2009, severity 1.0 — the same matrices used for the ΔE figures in the audit). Left column: current styling. Right column: proposed styling — colour-safe palettes plus redundant non-colour cues (labels, dashes, symbols, textures). ΔE is the CIELAB colour difference between two colours as seen under the simulation; below ~20 two colours are hard to tell apart at a glance, below ~10 they are effectively identical.

1 · Category shares (CatPlot) frontend/src/CatPlot.jsx

What to look for: under the deuteranopia filter, the current Set1 red, green, and orange segments all collapse into similar olive tones and the legend stops working — category identity is conveyed by colour alone (row and segment labels are hidden). The proposed version swaps in a worst-case-optimised Okabe-Ito subset (worst simulated pair ΔE 24.8 vs Set1's 10.0), shows the geography row labels, and prints percentages on the wider segments so colour is no longer the only decoder.

2 · Grouped comparison bars (OrdPlot) frontend/src/OrdPlot.jsx

What to look for: the focal green / reference magenta pair is itself CVD-safe (simulated ΔE ≥ 25 in every condition — it reads roughly as yellow-vs-blue to red-green-blind users), but the bars differ by colour only, which fails WCAG 1.4.1 and breaks in greyscale print. The proposed version keeps the hues and adds a diagonal-hatch decal plus a dark border to the reference series: the pairs read as "plain vs textured" without relying on colour at all.

3 · Time series (LongitudinalPlot) frontend/src/LongitudinalPlot.jsx

What to look for: currently both lines are solid, width 2, with identical circle symbols — colour is the only differentiator. The proposal makes the reference line dashed with triangle symbols, and darkens the focal green from #00e64d (1.69:1 against white — fails the 3:1 WCAG graphics minimum) to #00b33c.

4 · Distribution histogram (NCPlot) frontend/src/NCPlot.jsx

What to look for: the focal and reference markers are both dashed width-2 vertical lines with labels turned off — indistinguishable except by hue (the coloured text under the real chart is the only decoder). The proposal makes the focal line solid and slightly heavier, keeps the reference dashed, and turns on end labels so each line names itself.

Not shown here: map findings

The choropleth decile-ramp compression, the legend/map mix-blend-mode mismatch, and the focal outline contrast failure are map-rendering issues that don't reproduce meaningfully in static chart SVGs — see §2.3–§2.5 of the written audit for those, including all measured figures.

This page is fully self-contained (no scripts, no external requests) and prints cleanly. Simulation filters are SVG feColorMatrix operating in linear RGB. Charts were rendered server-side with the project's own ECharts 6.0.0.
